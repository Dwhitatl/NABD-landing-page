5.1 What membership costs

NABD offers a single membership tier: Adult Membership at $175.00 per United States dollar year, for individuals aged 18 and over. There is no youth, student, senior, or discounted tier.

5.2 Automatic renewal — please read before you enroll

Automatic renewal terms
  • Recurring charge. Your membership is an automatically renewing annual subscription.
  • Amount. $175.00 per year.
  • Frequency. Once every 12 months, on the anniversary of your enrolment date.
  • Duration. Renewals continue indefinitely until you cancel.
  • Payment method. The card or payment method you provide at checkout will be charged automatically each year without further action by you.
  • How to stop it. Cancel at any time using the methods in Section 6. Cancelling before your renewal date prevents the next charge.

Your membership year begins on the date your first payment is successfully processed and runs for twelve months. Unless you cancel before the end of that period, NABD will charge the payment method on file $175.00 to begin a new twelve-month term, and will continue to do so each year.

5.3 Renewal reminders

NABD will send a reminder to the email address associated with your membership before each annual renewal charge. The reminder will state the renewal amount, the date the charge will be made, and how to cancel. It is your responsibility to keep your email address and payment details current so that you receive these notices.

5.4 Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, NABD or its payment processor may attempt the charge again. If payment cannot be collected, your membership and its benefits may be suspended or ended. NABD will make reasonable efforts to notify you before that happens.
